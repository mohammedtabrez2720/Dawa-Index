🚧 Coming Soon — Currently Under Development

Our Vision for Dawa AI

Dawa AI will be a source-grounded medicine information assistant. Every answer will be traced to Dawa Index data and openFDA sources — never invented. It will help users understand pharmaceutical information safely and transparently.

Source-Grounded Answers

Every answer will show its source — Dawa Index database or openFDA. No guessing, no hallucination.

Safety First

Dawa AI will never diagnose, prescribe, or recommend starting or stopping any medicine.

Bilingual Support

Explanations available in English and Hindi, with Marathi planned for the future.

Full Transparency

A "Why this answer?" button will show exactly what information was used.

Planned Features

  Ask About Any Medicine

Type a question like "What is Atorvastatin?" and receive a clear, sourced explanation.

  Simple & Professional Modes

Choose plain-language explanations or structured professional information.

  Automatic Source Attribution

Every answer will display where its information came from with a timestamp.

  Medical Safety Guardrails

Clinical advice requests will be redirected to qualified healthcare professionals.

  Feedback & Reporting

Users can rate answers and report incorrect or outdated information.

  "Ask Dawa AI" on Drug Pages

A button on every drug card to start a Dawa AI conversation about that medicine.

Stay Tuned
